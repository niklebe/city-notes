# Rotating Tones: the worker draws a per-language shortlist and the model picks the best fit

Jokes are not written in one fixed voice. Each Analysis call receives, per language, a shortlist of three Tones drawn at random by the worker from a closed, versioned Tone list (excluding the Tones most recently used in that language and any not eligible for it), and the model picks the best fit for each language independently, returning its choice as `joke_tone_el` and `joke_tone_en`. We chose this over a single fixed voice because the Owner wants the gallery to be "all over the place", over "vary your tone" in the prompt because models collapse onto two or three favourites, and over a Tone forced by the worker because a forced Tone can fit the photo badly. Tones are tagged `gentle` or `any`; every shortlist contains at least one gentle Tone, and for `dangerous` Severity the model must pick a gentle one. The Tone list, system prompt and output schema share one `prompt_version`, stored on every Analysis row, so the cost of changing any of them is a new version, not a migration. Full contract: `docs/spec/analysis-prompt-contract.md`. It extends ADR 0001 and ADR 0004.
